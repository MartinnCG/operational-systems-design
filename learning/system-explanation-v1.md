# How to Explain the Portfolio as One System

## The thirty-second explanation

The portfolio explores how operational data becomes defensible evidence and how
AI can assist interpretation without replacing provenance or human authority.
Edge and geospatial projects produce different kinds of evidence. A shared
contract preserves origin, time, integrity and limitations. The governed AI lab
can interpret supported claim types, while deterministic controls and human
review remain outside the model.

## Why separate repositories?

They represent different responsibilities and rates of change. A GIS pipeline,
an edge ledger and an AI control layer should not share one release merely
because they participate in the same workflow. The contract connects them while
allowing each to be tested, replaced and explained independently.

## Why a common contract?

Without it, every integration becomes a one-off translation and the consumer
must know every producer's internal format. The envelope provides a stable
public boundary for identity, provenance, facts, scope and integrity. It does
not pretend that every domain has the same meaning.

## Why keep controls outside the model?

Language models are probabilistic. They may produce fluent but unsupported
claims. The architecture allows a model to propose typed assertions, then uses
deterministic validation to check citations and values. The model cannot grant
itself tools, broaden evidence scope or authorize a physical action.

## What is the creative contribution?

The contribution is the composition:

- field and edge evidence;
- geospatial evidence by operational segment;
- versioned cross-system provenance;
- governed AI interpretation;
- explicit limitations and human authority.

The components are familiar individually. The design value lies in deciding
how they cooperate without hiding uncertainty or collapsing their boundaries.

## Interview questions to master

1. What problem does each repository solve independently?
2. Why is the envelope owned centrally rather than copied informally?
3. What happens when a producer changes internally?
4. Why does a common envelope not imply common domain meaning?
5. How is simulated evidence prevented from becoming an observed claim?
6. What authority does the model have, and what authority is withheld?
7. How can a reviewer trace a final claim back to a source artifact?
8. Why is Crypto Alpha OS not connected yet?

## Honest authorship

AI assistance can accelerate implementation, drafting and testing. Architectural
ownership requires being able to explain the boundaries, alternatives,
trade-offs and non-claims. The goal is not to present every line as manually
typed; it is to make every published system decision reviewable and defensible.
